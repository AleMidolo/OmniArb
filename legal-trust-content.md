# Feature Specification — OMNI-006 Legal, Trust, Eligibility and Risk Content

**Priority:** P0  
**Status:** READY FOR ARCHITECTURE  
**Note:** This is a product specification, not legal advice. Final commercial copy requires appropriate legal/privacy review.

---

## 1. Objective

Ensure OmniArb communicates its commercial offer, eligibility, service boundaries and arbitrage risks clearly enough that the marketing funnel does not rely on misleading performance claims or hidden subscription conditions.

---

## 2. Confirmed product rules to communicate

### Eligibility

- 18+ only.
- B2C/private-consumer offer for the initial launch.
- Commercial availability limited to Italy.
- Exact legal eligibility test for "Italy only" is deferred to legal review.

### Service scope

OmniArb:
- provides arbitrage opportunity alerts;
- delivers ongoing service through Telegram;
- does not place bets;
- is not a bookmaker;
- does not hold customer betting funds;
- does not guarantee alert frequency;
- does not guarantee realized customer profit.

### Arbitrage explanation

The product may explain that a correctly executed arbitrage can mathematically lock in a positive theoretical result when all required wagers are accepted at the quoted odds and remain valid.

The experience must also communicate execution risks, including odds changes, stake limitations/rejections, voided bets/markets, bookmaker rules and user execution error.

### Commercial terms

- Seven-day full-access trial.
- Payment method required upfront.
- No €50 subscription charge at activation.
- Automatic conversion to €50/month recurring unless cancelled.
- One trial per customer.
- Trial cancellation prevents charge but preserves access through trial end.
- Paid cancellation stops renewal but preserves access through current paid period.
- Voluntary seven-day no-questions-asked refund guarantee on the first €50 payment only.
- Approved voluntary refund ends entitlement.
- Three-day grace period for failed recurring payment.
- Email reminder approximately 24–48 hours before first charge.
- Self-service subscription management with support fallback.

### Customer support

Initial support channel: email only.

Exact support address is a pre-launch configuration item.

## 3. Seller identity

The legal seller identity has **not yet been defined**.

Do not invent:
- legal name;
- business name as a legal entity;
- VAT number;
- registered address;
- legal contact details.

Commercial launch is blocked until required seller information is supplied and legally reviewed.

---

## 4. Required legal/customer-information areas

Before commercial launch, reviewed Italian content must cover as applicable:

- seller identification/contact information;
- terms of service;
- privacy information;
- cookie/analytics information where required;
- subscription price and recurrence;
- payment/trial timing;
- cancellation;
- voluntary refund guarantee;
- mandatory statutory consumer rights;
- age eligibility;
- geographic availability;
- support;
- Telegram/service access conditions;
- personal/non-shareable subscription;
- arbitrage/service-risk disclosures;
- limitations of scope;
- data processing related to email, Telegram identity, billing metadata and analytics.

The final structure of legal pages is not prescribed by this product specification.

---

## 5. Trust/content policy

### Permitted trust mechanisms

- clear explanations;
- transparent pricing;
- transparent cancellation/refund policy;
- genuine anonymized screenshots supplied by the product owner;
- explanatory mockups;
- static mathematical examples;
- accurate statement of service boundaries.

### Not permitted at launch

- fabricated testimonials;
- invented customer counts;
- unsupported success rates;
- unsupported profit statistics;
- unconditional guaranteed-profit claims;
- a guaranteed number of alerts;
- pretending that representative bookmaker/sport coverage is permanent.

---

## 6. Pre-commercial "Prossimamente" requirements

When the product is informational only:
- the future offer may be explained;
- the site must not imply trial activation is currently available;
- CTA must clearly say "Prossimamente";
- no subscription payment method is collected;
- no waiting-list/marketing-email capture is introduced.

---

## 7. Acceptance criteria

1. All customer-facing commercial/legal content required for launch is available in Italian.
2. The site clearly states the 18+ rule in the approved legal manner.
3. The site clearly communicates Italy-only commercial availability using the legally approved eligibility rule.
4. The service is not described as a bookmaker or automatic betting system.
5. The site does not claim unconditional guaranteed realized profit.
6. The site explains meaningful execution risks.
7. The site does not guarantee a minimum number of alerts.
8. The site communicates the seven-day trial and €50/month recurring conversion before activation.
9. The site communicates the voluntary first-payment refund policy without implying that it limits statutory rights.
10. The site provides access to required privacy/terms/customer information before commercial activation.
11. The seller's real legal identity is present before commercial activation.
12. The support email is present before commercial activation.
13. Real screenshots are anonymized and not presented as guaranteed-performance evidence.
14. No fabricated testimonial or performance evidence appears.
15. While commerce is disabled, the site presents "Prossimamente" rather than an active trial/payment action.
16. Legal/privacy review is recorded as complete before commercial activation.
